Leave Policy:
Employees receive 24 paid leave days per year.
Employees must request leave through their manager.
Emergency leave should be reported as soon as possible.

Work From Home Policy:
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
Work from home requires manager approval.
Employees must remain available during working hours.

Performance Policy:
Employee performance reviews happen twice per year.
Performance is evaluated based on goals, teamwork, and quality of work.
